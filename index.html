How to Connect & Print on Campus

Using Print Queue Names
Every printer on campus has a unique Print Queue Name (for example, HL0101 in Hutchins Library or DR0101 in Draper).
  1. Find Your Building: Click a building on the map or choose it from the dropdown list on the left.
  2. Copy the Queue Name: Click the Copy Queue button on the printer card for the room closest to you.
  3. Select Printer on Your Computer: Search for that Queue Name when adding a campus printer or releasing your job via PaperCut.
  4. Need Assistance? Contact Berea College Print, Mail & Design or the IS&S Help Desk and reference the Building, Room, and Print Queue Name shown on the card.
